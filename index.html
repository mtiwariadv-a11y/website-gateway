Professional Profile

Welcome to LawNat. Founded by Manoj Kumar Tiwari, Advocate, this platform serves as an informational resource and professional portfolio based out of New Delhi. The focus of the practice encompasses appellate litigation, regulatory analysis, international legal frameworks, and research-driven scrutiny of statutory and judicial precedents.

Jurisdictions & Practice Venues

Handling legal advisory, documentation, and appearances across the following forums and judicial panels in India:

Supreme Court of India • High Court of Delhi & State High Courts • National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) • National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT) • National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC) • National Green Tribunal (NGT) • District & Sessions Courts

Areas of Practice

International Law

Analysis of cross-border treaties, public international legal principles, diplomatic conventions, and jurisdictional coordination under private international legal frameworks.

Refugee & Asylum Law

Research and legal assistance concerning international refugee conventions, UNHCR mandate determinations, human rights protections, and non-refoulement doctrines.

Appellate Litigation

Drafting special leave petitions, statutory appeals, writ petitions, and formal pleadings before higher appellate courts and tribunals.

Corporate & Insolvency Law

Advisory regarding proceedings under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), company petitions, and administrative disputes before the NCLT and NCLAT.

Consumer Protections

Advocacy, advisory, and dispute documentation under the Consumer Protection Act before District Commissions, State Commissions, and the NCDRC.

Civil & Criminal Law

Drafting of legal notices, plaints, written statements, commercial contracts, bail applications, and trial representation across competent courts of record.
